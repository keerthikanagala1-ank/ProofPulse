When digital evidence becomes easier to create, knowing how to question it becomes essential.

Evidence deserves more than a glance.

A screenshot, receipt, certificate, or notice can look entirely convincing without actually proving the claim attached to it. Formatting, fonts, and familiar layouts create a sense of authority that has little to do with whether something is true.

ProofPulse doesn't decide what's real. It gives you a structured way to look closer — separating what a document visibly shows from what it can actually establish, so you know exactly what still needs to be checked.
